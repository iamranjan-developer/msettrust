Apply for Affiliation

For schools, ITIs, NGOs and training institutes — partner with MSET's free-education network.

Who Can Apply

  • Schools — admit MSET bridge-school students to formal classes
  • ITIs / Polytechnics — seat-share for meritorious MSET students
  • NGOs — co-run centres in new blocks and districts
  • Training Institutes — certify our vocational batches

Affiliation Process

  • Submit the form with organization details
  • Verification call + document check (7 days)
  • MOU signing and centre naming
  • Joint banner, shared reporting, annual review

Legal details of MSET are on the Legal & Affiliation page.
