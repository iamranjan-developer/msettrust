About MSET

10 Years of Empowering Through Education

Registered in 2015, MSET runs 48 learning & skill centres across 15 districts, focused on girls, dropouts and unemployed youth.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: Free, quality education and job-linked skill training for every underprivileged child and woman.

Vision: An India where no talent is wasted due to poverty.

  • Free bridge schools & tuition for Classes 1-12
  • Women-only skill batches with childcare support
  • Placement cell + micro-business seed help
  • Scholarships for college & ITI students
