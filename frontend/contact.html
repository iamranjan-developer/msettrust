FAQs

Is this trust government registered?

Yes, Modern Skill Educational Trust is legally registered and recognized. It holds valid government registrations including a verified NGO Darpan ID, Trust Deed, PAN card, official Bank Account, ISO certification, and official 80G & 12A registrations for tax exemption benefits.

Is the certificates and projects under this trust valid all over India and outside India?

Yes, the certificates and training programs provided under our trust are valid across India. Since our trust is legally registered, ISO certified, holds a verified NGO Darpan ID, Trust Deed, PAN, and valid 80G & 12A certifications, our courses and skill certificates are widely accepted for jobs, self-employment, and corporate requirements pan-India. For validity outside India, our certificates serve as genuine skill credentials and experience proof, though international acceptance may depend on specific corporate guidelines, credential evaluations (such as WES where applicable), or embassy attestations required by destination countries.

Is this trust authorized to accept donations and issue valid donation receipts?

Yes, our trust is legally authorized to accept donations through our official bank account and issue valid receipts. We are fully registered under 80G and 12A (along with Trust Deed, PAN, and NGO Darpan ID), which allows our donors to claim tax exemption benefits under the Indian Income Tax Act. Every donation received is properly documented, and official receipts are issued immediately.

How many fields or areas do the activities of this trust cover?

The activities of our trust cover multiple key fields backed by proper legal and institutional compliance (ISO certified, NGO Darpan registered). These include free primary education, skill training and vocational courses (such as computer, tailoring, beautician, and electrician), nursery schools and coaching centers, arts, music and dance academies, abacus and Vedic maths, spoken English centers, as well as health camps, child welfare, and community development programs across India.

Do you provide free franchises for multiple proprietary brands owned and created by your trust all over India?

Yes, we proudly offer free franchises across India for multiple proprietary brands and educational programs exclusively developed and owned by our trust. Backed by our official ISO certification, Trust Deed, NGO Darpan ID, and legal registrations, this includes our established networks for computer institutes, nursery schools, coaching centers, music and dance academies, abacus and Vedic maths centers, and spoken English institutes, allowing partners nationwide to establish and run these recognized brands without any franchise fees.

Is there any hidden cost or royalty fee in your free franchise program?

No, our franchise program is completely free of cost. We do not charge any hidden franchise fees, upfront security deposits, or royalty fees from our center partners. Our goal is to expand education and skill development across India without financial burdens.

Who can apply for the franchise center?

Any enthusiastic individual, educational entrepreneur, existing coaching center owner, teacher, or NGO worker who has a dedicated space (classroom/room), necessary seating arrangements, and a passion for spreading education can apply for our free franchise.

What kind of support is provided to franchise partners?

We provide complete support to our franchise partners, including course curriculum, study materials, student admission guidelines, software support, marketing guidance, and valid certificate generation upon course completion, backed by our official ISO certification and legal registrations.

Can students from any state in India join your courses?

Yes, students from any state, district, or village across India can join our online or offline courses and training programs. Our certifications are recognized and accepted pan-India.

How can a student or partner contact the trust for support?

Students and potential partners can easily reach out to us through the contact form on our official website, via email, or by calling our official phone numbers listed on the contact page. Our support team is available from Monday to Saturday.
