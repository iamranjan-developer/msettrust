Our Project

Running Projects

Six programs currently active across 48 centres in 15 districts — all free for eligible students.

Education

Bridge Schools (Class 1–8)

Dropout children rejoin formal school. Free books, uniforms and mid-day support. 22 centres running.

Women

Women Skill Centres

Tailoring, beautician and food-processing batches with on-site childcare. 14 centres running.

Youth

Job-Linked Training

Electrician, solar technician and spoken English with placement cell support. 6 centres running.

Health

Health & Hygiene Camps

Monthly camps, sanitary kit distribution and first-aid training for girls. 10 locations.

Education

Scholarships

College and ITI fee support for meritorious poor students — 120 scholarships this year.

Relief

Emergency Relief

Flood and winter relief — books, blankets and food kits when disaster strikes.
