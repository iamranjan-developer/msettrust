Associate Member

Give a few hours a month — help with teaching, events, field work or professional services.

What Associates Do

  • Weekend teaching support at nearby centres
  • Event help — admission drives, health camps, annual days
  • Field visits and student home follow-ups
  • Professional help — legal, design, accounts, media (pro bono)

Who Can Join

Anyone above 18 years with time to give. Students, professionals, retirees and homemakers are all welcome. No membership fee.

Associates receive an MSET ID card and a service certificate after 1 year.
