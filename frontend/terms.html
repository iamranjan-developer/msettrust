Terms & Conditions

Simple rules for students, donors and volunteers.

1. Free courses

All listed courses are 100% free. No admission or exam fee. Seats are limited (30 per centre) and given on eligibility + first-come basis.

2. Donations

Donations are voluntary. 80G receipts are emailed with PAN details. Wrong-amount refunds are processed within 15 working days on written request.

3. Website content

Photos and text may be updated by the admin team anytime. External links (maps, WhatsApp) follow their own policies.

4. Contact

For disputes, write to info@msettrust.org or visit our Sabang office. We resolve every complaint within 7 days.
