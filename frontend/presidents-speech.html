Ramesh Verma

Education is the Only True Asset

"When I started MSET in 2015 with a single room and twelve children in Sabang, people asked why I left teaching. The answer was simple — the children who needed a teacher the most had none.

Ten years later, that single room has become 48 centres across 15 districts. 12,500+ students — most of them girls, dropouts and unemployed youth — have learned to read, earn and dream. None of this was possible without donors who trusted us with every rupee, and with volunteers who gave their evenings.

We promise the same thing today that we promised in 2015: every child taught free, every rupee accounted for, every receipt honest. Education is the only asset that multiplies when you give it away — help us give more."
